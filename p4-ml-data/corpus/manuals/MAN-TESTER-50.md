## 1 Safety
Switch off the tester and unplug the measuring head before opening the electronics cabinet.

<!-- page 7 -->
## 2 Description
End-of-line torque tester. A rotary table (bearing 6001-2RS, SKF-6001) presents each drill to the measuring head; a
fixture (FIX-TT50) holds it. The measuring head is fed by a dedicated power supply (PSU-TT50).

<!-- page 11 -->
## 3 Sensors and normal values
| Signal | Normal | Warning | Trip |
|---|---|---|---|
| Table vibration RMS | 0.5–0.7 mm/s | 1.2 mm/s | 1.3 mm/s |
| Head / PSU temperature | 38–42 °C | 54 °C | 62 °C |

<!-- page 18 -->
## 7.1 Measuring head overheating
A rising head temperature and current with normal vibration means the power supply PSU-TT50 is overheating (dried-out
capacitors). Readings start to drift and good parts are rejected. Replace the PSU-TT50. Required skill: electrical.
Typical repair time 1 h.

<!-- page 20 -->
## 7.2 Fixture wear (misalignment)
Rising table vibration with normal kurtosis and more false rejects mean the fixture is worn and parts sit off-centre.
Replace the fixture FIX-TT50 and re-zero. Required skill: alignment. Typical repair time 1 h.

<!-- page 22 -->
## 7.3 Rotary table bearing wear
Rising table vibration with kurtosis above 4 means the rotary table bearing is wearing. Replace with SKF-6001.
Required skill: bearing_replacement. Typical repair time 1.5 h.

## 9 Spare parts
PSU-TT50 power supply · FIX-TT50 test fixture · SKF-6001 rotary table bearing.
