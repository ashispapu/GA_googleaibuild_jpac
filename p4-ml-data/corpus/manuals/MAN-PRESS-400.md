## 1 Safety
Lower the ram onto the safety blocks, stop the hydraulic pump and release the accumulator pressure (gauge must show
0 bar) before opening any line. Lock out the main isolator. Hydraulic oil can be above 60 °C after a run.

<!-- page 10 -->
## 2 Description
400 kN hydraulic press for housing assembly. A 22 kW motor drives the axial piston pump (1,450 rpm). Oil is cooled by
an oil-to-air cooler (HYD-CLR-400) and filtered by a 10 µm return filter (HYD-FLT-10). The ram runs in adjustable
gib guides (PRS-GDE-KIT).

## 3 Sensors and normal values
| Signal | Normal | Warning | Trip |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pump vibration RMS | 1.4–1.8 mm/s | 3.0 mm/s | 3.4 mm/s |
| Pump motor temperature | 58–62 °C | 74 °C | 82 °C |
| Pump bearing temperature | 50–54 °C | 66 °C | 75 °C |
| Motor current | 36–40 A | 44 A | 48 A |

## 5 Preventive maintenance
Every 1,000 h: change the return filter HYD-FLT-10, clean the cooler fins. Every 4,000 h: oil analysis, check the gib
clearance (max 0.05 mm), regrease the pump motor bearings (SKF-6308).

<!-- page 48 -->
## 7.2 Oil overheating
A rising motor temperature and motor current with normal vibration means the hydraulic oil runs hot: the pump works
against more internal leakage. Most often the oil cooler HYD-CLR-400 is fouled or its fan has failed. Clean the fins;
if the temperature difference across the cooler stays below 8 °C, replace the cooler. Hot oil above 82 °C ages seals
quickly. Required skill: hydraulics. Typical repair time 2 h. See procedure 8.1.

<!-- page 51 -->
## 7.3 Pump motor bearing wear
Rising vibration with kurtosis above 4 at the pump motor points to the motor bearing (6308-2Z). Replace with
SKF-6308. Required skill: bearing_replacement. Typical repair time 2.5 h. See procedure 8.3.

<!-- page 53 -->
## 7.5 Ram guide misalignment
Rising vibration with normal kurtosis, together with uneven press depth (quality: press_depth out of tolerance), means
the ram guides are worn and the ram runs out of line. Replace the gibs with PRS-GDE-KIT and readjust the clearance to
0.02–0.04 mm. Required skill: alignment. Typical repair time 3 h. See procedure 8.4.

## 8.1 Replacing the oil cooler (HYD-CLR-400)
1. Safety per section 1. 2. Close the cooler isolation valves, drain the cooler into a clean container. 3. Unbolt the
cooler (4× M10) and disconnect the fan. 4. Fit HYD-CLR-400 with new O-rings, open the valves, bleed. 5. Run the pump
30 min: oil temperature must settle below 50 °C and motor temperature below 64 °C.

<!-- page 64 -->
## 8.3 Replacing the pump motor bearing (SKF-6308)
1. Safety per section 1. 2. Uncouple the motor from the pump. 3. Remove the non-drive end shield. 4. Pull the bearing,
fit the new SKF-6308 heated to 80 °C. 5. Realign the coupling (max 0.03 mm). 6. Run 15 min: vibration below 2.0 mm/s.

<!-- page 67 -->
## 8.4 Replacing the ram guides (PRS-GDE-KIT)
1. Ram on blocks, pressure off. 2. Remove the old gibs. 3. Fit the PRS-GDE-KIT gibs, set clearance 0.02–0.04 mm with
feeler gauges. 4. Cycle 20 strokes empty, then check press depth on 5 parts (25.0 ± 0.1 mm).

## 9 Spare parts
HYD-CLR-400 oil cooler · HYD-FLT-10 return filter · SKF-6308 pump motor bearing · PRS-GDE-KIT ram guide gib kit.
