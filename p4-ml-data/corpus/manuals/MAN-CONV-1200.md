## 1 Safety
Stop the conveyor and lock out the drive before touching the belt or rollers. Pull-cords stop the belt but do not
isolate it.

<!-- page 8 -->
## 2 Description
1.2 m belt conveyor between gearbox assembly and end-of-line test. 1.5 kW drive motor (MTR-DRV-15), drive roller on
two 6204-2RS bearings (SKF-6204), tracking rollers (BLT-TRK-KIT).

## 3 Sensors and normal values
| Signal | Normal | Warning | Trip |
|---|---|---|---|
| Drive vibration RMS | 1.6–2.0 mm/s | 3.4 mm/s | 3.9 mm/s |
| Drive motor temperature | 45–50 °C | 62 °C | 70 °C |

## 7.1 Belt tracking (misalignment)
Rising drive vibration with normal kurtosis and parts arriving damaged at the tester mean the belt runs off-track and
rubs against the side frame. Adjust the tracking rollers; if the rollers are worn replace them with BLT-TRK-KIT.
Required skill: alignment. Typical repair time 1 h. See procedure 8.1.

<!-- page 24 -->
## 7.2 Drive roller bearing wear
Rising vibration with kurtosis above 4 at the drive end means a drive roller bearing is wearing. Replace both bearings
with SKF-6204. Required skill: bearing_replacement. Typical repair time 2 h. See procedure 8.2.

<!-- page 26 -->
## 7.3 Drive motor overheating
A rising motor temperature and current with normal vibration means the drive motor is overheating (winding
insulation ageing or a blocked fan cover). Replace the motor with MTR-DRV-15. Required skill: electrical. Typical
repair time 2 h. See procedure 8.3.

<!-- page 30 -->
## 8.1 Adjusting the belt tracking (BLT-TRK-KIT)
1. LOTO. 2. Fit the new tracking rollers from BLT-TRK-KIT. 3. Run empty at 30% and adjust the snub roller until the
belt stays centred within 5 mm. 4. Run loaded for 15 min.

<!-- page 32 -->
## 8.2 Replacing the drive roller bearings (SKF-6204)
1. LOTO, release belt tension. 2. Remove the drive roller. 3. Replace both SKF-6204 bearings. 4. Refit, re-tension,
check tracking.

<!-- page 34 -->
## 8.3 Replacing the drive motor (MTR-DRV-15)
1. LOTO, disconnect the motor. 2. Swap the motor MTR-DRV-15, check rotation direction. 3. Run 20 min: motor
temperature below 52 °C.

<!-- page 38 -->
## 9 Spare parts
BLT-TRK-KIT tracking roller kit · SKF-6204 drive roller bearing · MTR-DRV-15 drive motor.
